## Original text

I’m seeing a lot of folks looking into Fardriver controllers. I just want to say that these are not like other controllers and you can break your whole system guessing settings. I see more wrong answers than right ones about these controllers. If you are setting one up I suggest joining this group. The featured posts (especially for new users) are the best guide to these controllers out there. I am in several carrier groups and this one seems to produce correct answers more than others. Hit me up if you have any questions. I have been using them for a year and put them on everything I have now.

Feature 2: field weakening. When a motor spins it is pushed by using magnetic force to push or pull it in the desired direction. At the same time the motor spinning is creating a competing magnetic force. FD controllers use some kind of trick I don’t understand to weaken the opposing force. When your motor hits its top speed it is limited by the opposing force. Removing some of those effects gives you a higher top speed.
