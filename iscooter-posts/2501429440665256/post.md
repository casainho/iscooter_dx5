Good morning, everyone. Which controller would you recommend for upgrading my iScooter DX5 along with a 72V battery? I'm torn between the Fardriver Mini (80A battery / 260A phase) and the JRAHK (45A battery). I'm wondering if the extra cost for the Fardriver Mini is really worth it, considering I've seen videos where the Fardriver Mini (100A battery) hit 61 mph, while the JRAHK (45A battery) hit 55 mph—which I think is a great result considering it uses half the battery current.
