Ok I think I found the issue with the red exclamation mark warning light. I believe it was the brake lever. There is a little blue button that gets depressed when you release the brake lever, and I am pretty sure that is the sensor. That will trigger the warning light if it is not pressed all the way in. When I released my left side brake lever, it wasn't releasing all the way out to press that button all the way in, which was causing the warning light. I just pressed and depressed the brake  lever a bunch of times and I didn't get any more warning lights. The lever is obviously spring activated, I'm just not sure how to adjust that.
