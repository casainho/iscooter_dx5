iScooter should really do something about the display miscalibration. Today we did a 50.8km ride measured with GPS and the display was showing 72km. 43% more than what we really did. Plus its really annoying that basically the speedometer is absolutely useless.
The other problem is the controller settings. Even if you ever upgrade your battery like i did to 46Ah because of the voltage cut-off settings they set, when i reach 36% and still have 16ah left i start to lose power drastically and when I reach 30% it basically just wont go faster than 5km/h.
