## Original text

So I wonder if I should finally start up my YouTube channel with a helmet camera and the audio would be the goofy stuff I say to entertain myself along the way. In a car, I much prefer to have the radio on at all times but just the opposite on the scooter. So I talk and sing to myself and the critters I encounter on the way. This morning was reminiscent of that classic game Frogger. Except there were many frogs and only me and I'm pretty certain that I missed all of them. There were a couple of close calls. That and the maybe small dog or large cat that ran right in front of me. And so far it's fun because no animals, other than myself, were harmed in the making of this video... And you might see a wreck, IDK. Maybe I'll take pictures of the damage later. Rest assured none of the pictures will be of the scooter, and only three. I just need to do some tightening if possible or find other solutions. And a better headlight is a definite necessity as soon as I can.
I'm cross posting so not everyone who sees this is in on the fact that I messed up and wrecked this morning. I didn't realize it at the time but I was overdriving my headlights and ended up having to make a rapid stop going into high water. No warning signs but there was a flash flood warning in effect. I shouldn't have been going so fast for the possible conditions, live and learn. I did get a better idea of my actual range because I had to detour more than once and never ended up pushing it. I'm very pleased with it so far.
