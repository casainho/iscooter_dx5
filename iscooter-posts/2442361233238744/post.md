## Original text

I haven't looked into it a lot yet, it's something that I won't be able to afford anytime soon but it's something I've thought about for down the road. Dual motors. I know that will require a different controller but I think my three battery setup should be fine.
Has anyone done a mod like this?
If I get more speed that's cool, but I live in the Ozarks, I mostly need it for climbing hills without losing much power or not losing it at all. I also wonder about the stability given the platform as well.
In before, if this isn't possible given the setup I'm ok with that, however I'm not modding the scooter with a different motor, etc. It's not a hobby for me, this is my mostly daily commuter vehicle until I can get something faster. The only reason I can afford the modest modifications I've already made is because I'm not buying gasoline nearly as much as I used to. The suggestions are cool and you can still make them, I can only do stuff that's cheap. 
And maybe I haven't looked into it enough, if it's cheaper to just swap everything out instead of doing another iScooter motor on the front I'm also open to that.
Note: I think the dual motor thing is sick AF regardless so that will weigh my opinion somewhat.
