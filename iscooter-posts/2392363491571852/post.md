Update.
Don’t go thus big. Too wide for stock wheels. Gets unstable at top speed.
My DX5 showed up today. Stayed stock long enough to verify the motor works. I already had some 120/70-10 Michelin City Grip 2 so they went on. They are much heavier than stock but I plan on doing power mods in the future.
They fit fine except for the fenders.
Rides super smooth. I never used the stock tires to compare but these are high speed moped tires so I bet they are better.
