The wheel axle is long enough; the issue was mainly on the right side because there was no spacer between the wheel and the fork to stop the bolt from over-tightening. If I tightened it enough to secure the wheel firmly, it would put stress on the fork and bend it inwards. I believe this is why the wheel wasn't properly centered, and—possibly as a result of that misalignment—the brake pads were rubbing against the brake disc. For now, the caliper is removed, but I will check this issue again once I reinstall it. With the washers I added, the fork sits comfortably without being stressed by forced bending.
