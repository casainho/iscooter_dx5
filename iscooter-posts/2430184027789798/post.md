## Original text

I know it was never designed to go 55mph but I need help eliminating my high speed wobble. Upping the stock tires pressure helped some but it’s still there.
Update - talking with some other people with fast DX5s, I have learned it’s related to the head angle. The DX5 has about a 18° head angle while a standard moped is closer to 27°.  Shallower allows for more nimble maneuvering but less high speed stability. I checked my Roadrunner Pro and it’s around 20° and my Ariel Rider X-Class 52v is 25°. I’ll do some testing at different angles before I make the more permanent welds. Major reason why I prefer steel frames is that I can modify them.
A 2nd issue is the fork offset. The DX5 has over 50mm of fork offset which is how much the forks are in front of the head tube. More means more nimble steering and less high speed stability.  Other vehicles have 40mm or less. Fork offset impacts the mechanical trail length and so does head tube angle.
