## Original text

So I finally got the splitter that I ordered. I'm running two OEM batteries in the deck and I have a friend that might be able to 3D print a cover for them but I'm going to be running "naked" for a while. I was originally going to drill a hole for the separate charge port but I changed my mind and think I'm going to install it in the new cover, the wires are long enough and although I hope to no longer have to access the batteries as much but I prefer the option instead of not having to open the entire deck every time so there will be a hinge and a way to snap it down.
And yes, that's a third input for one more battery. I want to put it where the "gas tank" goes so I want it to look somewhat like one. If I can find one that fits, for the third battery I want to go with 48v 20ah. 
I won't be changing anything that makes it a DX5, not throwing shade at anyone who does. But I might extend the swing arm and maybe put some short ape hangers on, a bit longer than the factory ones and after that I think all the aesthetics I want to do will be done.
And when I'm done and I start saving money instead of spending more on this mini beast I will save my pennies for a Goat Hulk and this will be my in town ride.
