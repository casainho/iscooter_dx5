## Original text

I just got my scooter today. I'm pretty sure I got a returned repackaged unit even though it stated "New" on the listing. Steps 1, 2 and 3 already had paint chipped and screws cross threaded before I got to either step. The shipping box was completely damaged when I got to it, so I never had a chance to confront FedEx directly. Extremely cheap cardboard that ripped to pieces where you grabbed it. One screw for the kickstand was bent right from shipping. It was a major hole in the side of the box. I managed to complete the assembly even with the manual's very fine print. I'm sure they'll deny that I received a repack, but too many stripped screws and paint chips to deny it. I have it charging and expect to give it a shot later today.
I'm looking forward to seeing what everyone does to personalize theirs.
Don't go assuming that I didn't make this purchase from a legitimate source. I can tell by the comments many think I didn't.
