## Original text

I started measuring the wires before converting my DX5 Pro scooter to the Fardriver Mini 12 controller. There’s a purple wire that controls the brake light. When I pull the brake lever relative to GND, it applies the full battery voltage—56 volts—to the rear brake light. My question is: can the Fardriver Mini 12’s “high brake” output handle this high current? Because I’ve read that it can handle a maximum of 12 volts. How have others solved the issue of getting the rear brake light to work?
