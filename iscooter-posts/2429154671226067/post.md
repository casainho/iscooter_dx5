## Original text

I'm certain that when I first got the scooter that I had a bit more range than I do now. It might be an actual mile less now. 
I use it as a daily commuter, 14 miles one way. If I ever have to detour the trip suddenly becomes "will I make it". I was aware very early on that I would need at least one more battery so I got one but have not connected it yet. The parts are in the mail, I'm pretty sure I ordered the right ones 
. iScooter will not give any support for this kind of modification, I know, I emailed them.
I guess this is my long winded way of asking, has anyone else noticed a reduction in range? I'd give the current mileage for reference but I don't know what it really is and I don't pay much attention to it. The last time I remember catching the "mileage": I definitely remember because it made me chuckle. 999 lol. It wasn't long ago but I don't remember when it was exactly. Oh, I've had it for about two months now although it was out of commission for a couple of weeks.
