If you want to extend the scooter's range, you can add a larger battery or connect a second battery in parallel. However, keep in mind that the OEM iScooter DX5 battery uses a BMS with separate charge and discharge ports. Because of this, the batteries cannot be charged together in parallel unless you first replace the OEM BMS with one that uses a common charge/discharge port. Otherwise, you'll need to charge each battery separately.
Also, before connecting two batteries in parallel, they should be at the same voltage (or as close as possible, ideally within a few tens of millivolts). Connecting batteries with different voltages can cause a very high equalization current to flow between them, which can damage the batteries, BMS, wiring, or connectors.
