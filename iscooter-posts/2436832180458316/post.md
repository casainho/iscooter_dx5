## Original text

Had some time to mod my head stem today. Cut part of the original off at ~8° and welded a new pipe on that remaining stock stem. I am a grinder and painter when it comes to welding. They hold but aren’t pretty. After the test run I just did I will be filling the welds.
I also lowered the new stem almost 2” to make up for the front end drop that would have happened with the new steeper head angle.
The scooter rides very different now. More like an adult sized bike where you have to actually lean a good bit to make a turn. Zero instability at over 50mph.
Next I’ll finish the welding, find the best matching spray paint I can, and put my 120/70-10 moped tires back on now that I know those were never the problem. Plus a few other things to button up.
BTW. I used ChatGPT to help with all the math for this mod.
