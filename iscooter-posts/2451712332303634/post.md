If you have recently changed the brake pads or the scooter is new, the hydraulic pistons might be extended too far outwards. The hydraulic brake pistons on the iScooter DX5 Pro (or any hydraulic system) may remain extended too far, causing the brake pads to rub against the disc due to specific physical and mechanical factors: The automatic self-adjustment mechanism (the most common cause). Modern hydraulic brakes feature a smart function: as the brake pads wear down and become thinner, the pistons automatically extend further to ensure the brake lever travel remains short and firm. How the problem arises: If you squeezed the brake lever—even briefly—while the caliper was removed from the disc or the wheel was taken off, the pistons "assumed" the pads had worn down. Consequently, they extended outward and became stuck in that new position, reducing the clearance for the disc. It’s possible someone pressed the brake before the rear wheel was installed. Or perhaps the system is defective from the factory. I’ll find out once I manage to remove the caliper and take out the pads to see if the pistons have extended and if I need to push them back into place.
