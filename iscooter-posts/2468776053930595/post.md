## Original text

Some background. I have the DX5 and DX5 pro. Both use the same exact upgraded controller and same line/phase amps, however, the DX5 is running 72v and the DX5 pro is running 108v.
I finally had time to open up the DX5 pro. The motor, although it looks the same as the DX5, has noticeably more low-end torque!! It pulls way harder when accelerating (the higher voltage only extends the torque into higher RPM but doesn't increase it). 
I suspect the DX5 pro motor is wound for a higher torque constant. Aside from the thin phase wires, that motor is shockingly capable for a stock motor, way more so than the regular DX5's motor.
To anyone who has the Pro model, upgrading the controller and battery is a MUST. It turns the little scooter into a weapon.
