start with the controller. all the diagrams are on their website. join this group 
https://www.facebook.com/groups/923856302074029 and read through the featured post section. focus on the stuff directed at beginners and new tunes. I read through all that and had no issues when i set up my first one. These things really only have four relevant parts and that's what you should focus on when you start. Get the throttle, battery, controller and motor hooked up and spinning then go back and start adding the other stuff one thing at a time. Keep your focus small so you can identify any issues one at a time. This way you only have one point of failure. This stuff is super easy once you are able to look at the wires and tell what stuff is. You probably won't need 50% of the connectors on the harness. Feel free to reach out if you have any quesitons.
