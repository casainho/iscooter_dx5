I just spent 10 hours fixing that exact issue on my DX5 Pro! It's the brake disc scraping against the brake pads. Do you have your spacers on the right way? Smaller spacer goes on the brake/disc side. Loosen the axle and if there's a u frame like the DX5 Pro, then push on the front forks and tighten again the axle then the fender/u shape. If it keeps scraping unscrew the 2 caliper bolts from the frame but not all the way. Keep em loose and push on the brake lever then while pushing the brake lever tighten back up the 2 caliper bolts. Hopefully that fixes the scraping from the brake disc rubbing against the brake pads. If not loosen the bolts again on the caliper and put 2 business cards, thin and put them in the little slots openings right by the brake pads then with the cards in there (can use playing cards too) hold the brake lever down again while tightening the caliper slowly, one then the other. When tight take the cards out and it should align everything and stop the scraping. I learned and learned through last night and today doing the exact same thing. Between those 3 ways it'll align the disc and brake pads and wheel to normal. Hope that helps.
