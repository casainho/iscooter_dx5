## Original text

I've made some changes to the lighting which I think make riding in traffic safer. 
First, the stock brake light is not very bright, but the running light is - so swap them for a much better brake light. 
Second, I added front turn signals. All the lighting is 48v LED, so that's what you are after. The lights I got claimed that they didn't need flashers, but they did, so you might need some of those too. 
You need to remove the taillight assembly and cut back the black case around 4" to allow tapping into the wires. Add 2 conductor stuff long enough the reach the front for the new turn signals. Solder all the joints & use heat shrink tubing. 
Easy peasy
